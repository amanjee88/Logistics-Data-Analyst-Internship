This report presents an exploratory data analysis (EDA) of a hypothetical logistics operation using Python. The purpose of the task is to turn shipment-level records into clear descriptive findings about delivery duration, delivery delay, transportation cost, regional reliability, vehicle-type differences, shipment volume, and relationships among numeric variables. A deterministic script generates 300 synthetic shipment records for this exercise. These are simulated teaching records, not data collected from a real logistics company, and all reported metrics and charts describe only this synthetic sample.

The analysis uses pandas and NumPy to validate the dataset, derive delivery-time and delay measures from timestamps, calculate summary statistics, and group operational indicators by region, vehicle type, and order week. Matplotlib and Seaborn are used to create seven visualizations: a delivery-time histogram, a cost-versus-distance scatter plot, a regional delay box plot, a mean cost-by-vehicle chart, an on-time rate-by-region chart, a Pearson correlation heatmap, and a weekly shipment-volume/on-time-rate chart. Each visualization is included in the report with an explanation of why it is suitable and what it suggests within the hypothetical scenario.

The results show a simulated overall on-time delivery rate of 63.3%, mean delivery time of 10.05 hours, median delivery time of 8.07 hours, mean transportation cost of INR 1,681.51 per shipment, and first-attempt success of 90.3%. The East region has the lowest simulated on-time rate at 40.0%, whereas South has the highest at 76.4%. Distance is strongly correlated with transportation cost in the generated data; this relationship is partly embedded in the simulation formula and must not be treated as independent real-world evidence. The report therefore recommends investigating regional and route-level patterns, reporting median and percentile delivery measures in addition to averages, comparing vehicle costs using suitable normalised metrics, and checking weekly sample sizes before interpreting variations.

The repository includes the dataset generator, EDA script, generated summary tables, chart images, data-quality checks, automated tests, and instructions to reproduce the results. Public sources are referenced for logistics context and visualization methods only; they do not supply or validate the synthetic shipment-level findings. No causal claims, operational savings, or real-company performance conclusions are made.
